How to read the card

  • Go out Darkness + a clearer window + enough activity that a look is worth it. Still not a guarantee.
  • Maybe Mixed signals. Fine if you’re already outside; don’t drive an hour on this card alone.
  • Stay in Daylight, socked-in clouds, or a quiet oval. Check again if Kp jumps.
  • Kp is a planetary 0–9 index (NOAA G-scale from Kp 5). It is not “% chance over your ZIP.”
  • OVATION % is the nearest 1° SWPC cell — precipitation probability, not “you will see it.”
  • Look is a horizon hint (N / S / overhead), not a camera azimuth.
